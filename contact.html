Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries based on our school records and policies.

How can I apply for admission at RACS?

We admit learners into Play Group, Pre-Primary (PP1 & PP2), Lower Primary (Grades 1–3), and Upper Primary (Grades 4–6). To apply, parents or guardians submit a completed application form accompanied by a copy of the learner’s birth certificate, recent passport-size photographs, and previous school transfer reports. Detailed requirements can be found on our Admissions Page or requested directly at our administration office.

How can I contact the school administration?

You can call or message our administration team at 0729 622 381 or 0724 855 225. You can also send an email to RACS2021@gmail.com. Our office is open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

What are the school tuition fees?

Official termly tuition fees are:

  • Play Group: KSh 6,000 per term
  • Pre-Primary (PP1 & PP2): KSh 8,500 per term
  • Lower Primary (Grades 1–3): KSh 11,000 per term
  • Upper Primary (Grades 4–6): KSh 13,000 per term

School meals (10 o'clock break & hot lunch) are KSh 4,500 per term. View the complete table on our Fee Structure page.

Does the school provide transport, and which routes are covered?

Yes, RACS operates school transport covering 7 designated operational zones including Mashuria, Rimpa, Nalepo, Kahuho Stage, Kamura, Kanisani, Destiny, Kwa Maji, Gichagi, Kiserian Town, QuickMart, and Kandisi. Termly charges range from KSh 4,000 for Zone 1 to KSh 8,500 for Zone 7. Full stage details are listed on our Transport Fees page.

How can I arrange a visit to the school?

Parents and guardians are warmly welcome to visit our campus in Rimpa, located approximately 10 metres from Cana Hospital off Magadi Road, Kajiado County. Our office is open on weekdays from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. To schedule a guided walkthrough of our classrooms and compound or meet with the Headteacher, please contact us by phone or WhatsApp at 0729 622 381.
