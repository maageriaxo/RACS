Admissions at RACS

Everything you need to know about joining Rimpa Adventist Comprehensive School.

We are delighted that you are considering RACS for your child. We admit learners into Play Group, Pre-school, and Primary levels, and we are committed to walking with families through every step of the journey.

Admission process

Step-by-step guide from first enquiry to joining the RACS family.

  1. Visit or contact the school office for initial enquiries and to receive admission information.
  2. Collect application forms and prospectus from the office or request soft copies via email or WhatsApp.
  3. Submit completed forms together with required documents (previous school reports, birth certificate, photos, etc.).
  4. Learners may be invited for an assessment or interview depending on the class level.
  5. Parents/guardians meet with the administration to confirm placement and discuss next steps.
  6. Upon confirmation, initial fees are paid and learners receive reporting dates and requirements.

Online application (current process)

Until full online forms are available, families can still apply remotely.

We are working towards a fully online admission system. For now, parents and guardians can start the process by contacting the school via phone or email to request soft copies of admission forms. Completed forms can be returned electronically or brought to the school office for processing.

For detailed guidance or to request forms, please use the contacts on our Contact & location section.

Admission & graduation requirements

Documents and expectations that guide learners from entry to completion.

Admission requirements

  • Completed application form signed by parent or legal guardian.
  • Copy of the learner’s birth certificate.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • Previous school reports or transfer documents (for continuing learners).
  • Any medical or special needs information that can help us support the learner well.

Graduation expectations

Learners are expected to complete the required academic work for their level, demonstrate good conduct, and participate in spiritual and co-curricular programmes. Graduation from RACS reflects not only academic achievement but also growth in character and service.
