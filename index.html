About RACS

Who we are, what we believe, and why families choose Rimpa Adventist Comprehensive School.

Rimpa Adventist Comprehensive School (RACS) is a Christian-based school that fosters a balanced development of the whole child: spiritual, physical, intellectual, and social-emotional. A process that spans a lifetime.

Why choose RACS

  • Commitment to academic excellence
  • Strong Christian values and character formation
  • Affordable, value-driven education
  • Talent nurturing through co-curricular activities

Vision

To be an institution of excellence that empowers learners of all abilities through holistic, faith based education that fosters Christ-centered, lifelong learners who serve with excellence, integrity, and compassion.

Mission

To provide a Christ-centered, inclusive, and holistic education that nurtures the spiritual, intellectual, physical and social development of every learner. Through dedicated service, academic excellence, and faith in God, to fulfil their God-given purpose and positively impact the world.

Academics

Learning that blends strong academics with Christian values and life skills.

Our academic programme follows the national curriculum, enriched with Christian values and life skills. Teachers provide close mentorship, continuous assessment, and support to ensure each learner reaches their God-given potential.

The school calendar outlines opening and closing dates, mid-term breaks, examination weeks, and special events such as sports days and cultural activities. Parents receive printed calendars and updates; we will also publish key dates on this page.

Daily timetables balance core subjects, creative activities, devotions, and co-curricular sessions. Sample timetables for different classes will be uploaded here so that families can easily follow their children’s learning routines.
