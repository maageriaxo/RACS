About Rimpa Adventist Comprehensive School

Who we are, what we believe, and why families choose RACS.

Rimpa Adventist Comprehensive School (RACS) is a Christian-based school that fosters a balanced development of learners in all areas: spiritual, intellectual, physical, cultural, and social-emotional. We strive to provide a safe, nurturing environment where every learner is encouraged to discover and develop their God-given talents.

Why choose RACS

What makes our school a special place for learners and families.

We are an Institution of:

  • Excellence: High academic standards and continuous learner mentorship.
  • Quality: Qualified, dedicated teachers and safe learning facilities.
  • Affordability: Value-driven education designed for local families.
  • Talent Nurturing: Co-curricular activities, languages (French), swimming, and leadership.

Admissions open for: Play-Group, Pre-school (PP1 & PP2), and Primary (Grades 1–6).

Our vision

The long-term direction that guides everything we do.

To be an institution of excellence that empowers learners of all abilities through holistic, faith based education that fosters Christ-centered, lifelong learners who serve with excellence, integrity, and compassion.

Our mission

How we live out our calling in everyday school life.

To provide a Christ-centered, inclusive, and holistic education that nurtures the spiritual, intellectual, physical and social development of every learner. Through dedicated service, academic excellence, and faith in God, to fulfil their God-given purpose and positively impact the world.
