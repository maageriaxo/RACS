Academics at RACS

A balanced programme that combines strong academics, faith, and life skills.

RACS follows the national curriculum and integrates Christian values across all learning areas. Our teachers mentor learners closely, monitor progress through continuous assessment, and encourage active participation in class and co-curricular activities.

School calendar

Key dates that guide learning, assessments, and special events.

Each academic year is organised into terms with clearly defined opening and closing dates, mid-term breaks, assessment weeks, and activity days. Parents receive printed calendars and SMS updates to keep them informed of important dates.

A detailed term-by-term calendar will be uploaded here, including exam weeks, sports days, cultural events, and parent–teacher meetings.
